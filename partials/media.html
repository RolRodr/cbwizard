Media Files (Optional)

Add images, audio, or PDF files to your collection.

Select media files for your collection (Images, Audio, PDF). Ideally, use URLs for large files in your CSV because GitHub has strict limits on file sizes. Files must not exceed 10 MB each.

Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, MP3, PDF. Each file must be under 10 MB.

CollectionBuilder-CSV requires small preview images. Check this to automatically generate them from your uploaded images and update your CSV.

Media files will be uploaded when you publish in the final step.
